## set up

- Can you help me set up this new computer?你能帮我设置这台新电脑吗？
- I am planning to set him up out of jealousy.（我打算设计陷害他，只因心生嫉妒。）

## show up

- Our manager didn't show up for the ISO audit meeting.
- The missing memory card finally showed up in an old box.那张遗失的存储卡最后在一个旧箱子里找到了。
- The lost memory card turned up inside an old box.
- He showed up the competition with his brilliant performance. 王扬以出色的表现胜过了竞争对手。

## speak up

- Could you please speak up?I can barely hear you on WeChat.
- It's important to speak up for what you believe in. 要敢于为自己秉持的立场仗义执言，这点很重要。

## split up

- I decided to split up with her after three years together.I’ve decided to break up with her.（最口语常用）
- The teacher asked the students to split up into pairs for the activity. The teacher told students to pair off for the task.

## stand up

- He is too weak to stand up.
- You need to stand up for what you believe in.你需要坚持你所相信的。
- The argument cannot stand up to scrutiny. 这套说辞经不起推敲。

## take up

- She decided to take up flower arranging in her free time.她打算闲暇时开始学习插花。
- My new job will take up most of my time.
- The tailor needs to take up the hem of these trousers.裁缝需要改短这条裤子的裤脚。
- She was quick to take up the challenge.她很快就接受了挑战。

## tangle up

- The earphone cords got tangled up.耳机线缠成一团了.
- I got tangled up in a relationship with a charming jinx. I’m caught up with someone who’s a lovely jinx.

## threw up

- I threw up from severe kidney stone pain.肾结石剧痛害得我呕吐了。
- The company managed to throw up a new website in just a few days.这家公司短短几天仓促赶工搭好了新官网。

## turn up

- Don't worry, your lost keys will eventually turn up.别担心，你丢失的钥匙最终会出现的。
- Turn up the heat, it's getting cold in here.把暖气开大点，这里变冷了。

## use up

- The long trek to BXJ really used up all my energy.长途跋涉去往 BXJ，着实耗尽了我全部精力。
- The car used up a lot of fuel on the long journey.这辆车在长途旅行中消耗了很多燃料。

## warm up

- The singer did some vocal exercises to warm up her voice.歌手做了一些声乐练习来热身她的嗓子。
- She slowly warmed up to working overtime.她慢慢接受加班这件事了。

## zip up

- Can you help me zip up my dress?你能帮我拉上裙子的拉链吗?
- I quickly zipped up my jacket before going outside.
